Point 3

House Rules as text instead of an image

In the design, the House Rules are a picture of your PDF. We suggest keeping the same look, the paper card over a photo, but writing the rules as real text on the website:

  • Easy to read on phones. Text in a picture shrinks with the picture, so visitors have to zoom in.
  • Found on Google. People search for things like vaccination rules. Google cannot read text inside a picture.
  • Quick to update. Changing one sentence no longer means editing in Canva and exporting a new image.
  • Shorter page. Each rule opens with a tap, so the card stays compact on the homepage.

Preview: tap a rule to open it

House Rules

At The Dog Club, we're all about creating a safe, happy, and respectful space where dogs can play, learn, and make new friends. Thank you for helping us keep our community fun for everyone.

  • Healthy Dogs Only

    Please only bring your dog if they're feeling well. Dogs showing signs of illness, injury, or contagious conditions should stay home until they're fully recovered.

  • Vaccinations Required

    All dogs must be up to date with their DHPP/DHPPL, Rabies, and Bordetella vaccinations before visiting The Dog Club. For your dog's safety, visits are permitted at least 7 days after their most recent vaccination.

  • Spayed & Neutered

    Dogs aged 6 months and older must be spayed or neutered to access our shared park areas. Female dogs in heat are not permitted. If your dog unexpectedly comes into heat during her stay, we'll do our best to keep her safe by separating her from other dogs. While we'll take every reasonable precaution, The Dog Club cannot be held responsible for any consequences resulting from an unexpected heat cycle.

  • Tick & Flea Prevention

    To help keep all dogs safe and our facilities pest-free, dogs must receive a tick and flea preventive treatment within 24 hours before check-in. Dogs found with ticks or fleas upon arrival may be refused entry until they have been treated.

  • Respect Every Dog

    Every dog has a different play style. If our team feels a dog needs a break, a different play group, or to leave the play area, we kindly ask that you follow our guidance. Our decisions are always made with the safety and wellbeing of all dogs in mind. As dog play is naturally social and unpredictable, owners remain responsible for any injuries or damages caused by their own dog.

  • Booking & Refunds Draft

    Registration is required before any booking can be made. New dogs complete an evaluation before joining daycare or boarding, and a boarding stay is only confirmed once you receive confirmation from our team.

    No refunds are issued for early check-outs. Any unused balance is converted into store credit, which can be used for future services.

Thank you for helping us create a fun, safe, and welcoming environment for every dog and every visit.
