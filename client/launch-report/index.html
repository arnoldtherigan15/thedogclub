Our recommendations

Biggest effect on local search

Google Business Profile for each branch

For searches like "dog daycare near me", Google mostly uses your Business Profiles (the cards on Google Maps). Keep one profile per branch, all owned by a company account, and make the name, WhatsApp number, hours and website link match the new website. For example, the Umalas profile is named "The Dog Club & Park", and the Ungasan WhatsApp number changed to +62 812-4645-3589. Asking happy customers for a Google review helps most.

Email security

Add an SPF record for your email

Your domain tells email providers to reject mail that cannot prove it comes from you, but it does not yet list Google as an allowed sender (an "SPF" record). Adding it is one line in the domain settings and makes it less likely that your emails land in spam. We can add it with your approval.

Later

Meta ads tracking

If your Instagram and Facebook ads later send people to the website, connecting the site to Meta (Meta Pixel) shows which ads bring WhatsApp chats. Your ads now go straight to WhatsApp, so this can wait, as agreed.
