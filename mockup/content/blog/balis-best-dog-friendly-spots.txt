Dog-Friendly Beaches in Bali
Bali offers a variety of stunning beaches where your dog can frolic in the sand and surf. Here are some of the top dog-friendly beaches on the island:
Echo Beach: Located in Canggu, Echo Beach welcomes dogs and their owners to enjoy the sun and waves together. Your furry friend can run off-leash and make new friends while you relax and soak up the beach vibes.
Sanur Beach: Sanur Beach is known for its calm waters, making it a perfect spot for dogs who enjoy a refreshing swim. Take a leisurely stroll along the beachfront promenade with your pup and enjoy the beachside cafes.
Batu Bolong Beach: Situated in the hip and vibrant area of Canggu, Batu Bolong Beach allows dogs on its shores. Watch the surfers catch waves while your furry friend explores the sandy shoreline.
Pet-Friendly Cafes and Restaurants in Bali
Bali’s bustling café scene has embraced pet-friendly policies, ensuring you can enjoy a meal or a cup of coffee without leaving your furry friend behind. Here are some pet-friendly cafes and restaurants in Bali:
Crate Café: Located in Canggu, Crate Café is a popular spot that welcomes dogs in its outdoor seating area. Treat yourself to their delectable brunch options while your dog lounges by your side.
Café Organic: With multiple locations in Seminyak, Ubud, and Canggu, Café Organic offers a relaxed and pet-friendly environment. Enjoy healthy and delicious meals while your pup receives special attention from the friendly staff.
Milk & Madu: Situated in Berawa, Milk & Madu is known for its vibrant atmosphere and scrumptious dishes. Your furry friend can join you on the outdoor terrace while you indulge in a hearty meal.
Pet-Friendly Hotels in Bali
If you’re looking for accommodation that welcomes both you and your furry friend, Bali has several pet-friendly hotels. Here are a few options:
The Trans Resort Bali: Located in Seminyak, The Trans Resort Bali welcomes pets and provides comfortable accommodation and amenities for both you and your dog. Enjoy a relaxing stay in their pet-friendly rooms and explore the nearby attractions.
The Oberoi Beach Resort, Bali: Situated in Seminyak, The Oberoi Beach Resort offers a luxurious pet-friendly experience. Pamper yourself and your pup with their top-notch facilities and beautiful beachfront location.
Maya Sanur Resort & Spa: Maya Sanur Resort & Spa in Sanur is another pet-friendly option that provides a tranquil and picturesque setting. Enjoy the resort’s hospitality while your dog receives special attention and care.
Mount Batur Hike
For the adventurous souls, the Mount Batur hike is a fantastic experience to share with your canine friend. Enjoy a sunrise trek together and witness breathtaking panoramic views from the summit. Just make sure your dog is fit for the hike and well-prepared with the necessary supplies.
