FAQs

Common questions before your dog's visit

Can't find your answer? We're here to help on WhatsApp.

How do I register my dog?

Register once on our booking portal (Pawsi). You'll fill in your details and your pup's, and upload the vaccination book. Registration is free and the information is for internal use only. See Join the Club for the full steps.

What types of boarding do you offer?

Two types: Deluxe and Prestige. Deluxe includes daily park access and a night at the Dog Villa, which has a pool and private bedrooms on request. Prestige adds a daily morning beach walk with friends. You'll receive highlights of your dog's day on WhatsApp.

Capacity is limited on purpose, so please book your slot in advance, especially for weekends.

What does a typical day at The Dog Club look like?
  • 7am: Wake up at the Dog Villa, relaxed and ready for the day.
  • 8am: Morning beach walk with the gang along Batu Belig beach (Prestige boarding).
  • 9am: Boarding doggies are brought from the Villa to the Park. Let the fun begin!
  • 9am – 5pm: Playtime at the Club, all dogs supervised by our trained staff.
  • 5pm: Well-deserved feeding time at the Park.
  • 6pm: Quick inspection, then back to the Dog Villa for a good night's rest.
Is it safe for my dog at The Dog Club?

Whenever several dogs share a space there are some risks, much like kids at school. Most parents agree the benefits of socialisation and exercise outweigh them. Through our policies, hygiene standards and trained staff we take every precaution to minimise risks, though they can't be eliminated entirely. Aggressive dogs are not allowed at the Club.

What happens if my dog gets into a fight or gets wounded?

Most dogs love to play vigorously, and minor cuts or scrapes can happen. Our certified staff monitor the dogs at all times, but no supervision can fully prevent injuries. Like most pet care facilities, The Dog Club does not cover veterinary charges for these situations. They are rare exceptions: most dogs go home happily tired, clean and healthy.

Do you have swimming pools for dogs?

Yes, three: two bone-shaped pools in the Park and one standard pool at the Dog Villa. Not a water dog? No problem, plenty of pups play while avoiding the splashes. We also offer swimming lessons in our shallow pool.

What are your grooming options?

Grooming, quick wash, haircut, style cut, pawdicure, massage and tick removal, all with natural products. Grooming must be booked in advance. See the Dog Spa page for prices.

Will you take my dog for a walk?

Yes. Dogs leave around 8am with our Dog Taxi and two staff walk the group on leash for one hour (3 – 4 km), usually along Batu Belig beach.

Can you train my cheeky pup?

A trained doggie is a happy doggie! Ask our team for a training appointment. Our trainer can work with your dog while they stay with us.

Do you have a taxi service for my dog?

Yes. Our fleet has a scooter, a dog tuk-tuk and a van. Under 2 km is free, 2 – 5 km is IDR 30.000 one way, 5 – 8 km is IDR 50.000 one way, and beyond 8 km our partner pet taxi takes over (price on request).

What about fleas and ticks?

Your dog must arrive free of fleas and ticks and be on flea and tick prevention. Every dog goes through a check-in inspection before entering the Park.

How will my dog behave after a stay?

Life at the Club is exciting! Dogs usually go home tired but happy and may sleep more than usual. Some get a little diarrhoea from the excitement, which is normal, and active dogs can lose a bit of weight from all the play. Feed them well after a big day.
