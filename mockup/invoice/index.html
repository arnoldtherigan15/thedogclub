What happens next

About 3 weeks from the down payment. Each step needs your approval before the next one starts.

  1. Week 1
    Brand alignment & design. We adapt the mockup to your brand (logo, colours, fonts, photos). You review the homepage and one service page on desktop and mobile. Up to 2 rounds of major revisions. → design approved, payment 2
  2. Week 2
    Build. All 27 pages are built on a private preview link that you can open on any device.
  3. Week 3
    Review & launch. You check content and prices on the preview link and send one list of corrections. After fixes and payment 3, the site goes live on thedogclub.id. → website live

What we need from you

To fit the design to your brand, please send these within 2 working days of the down payment.

  • Logo in the original file (AI, SVG, PDF or a large PNG with a transparent background).
  • Brand guidelines if you have them: colour codes, fonts, examples of posts or printed materials you like.
  • Photos of the park, villa, pools, spa, team and happy dogs (originals, not screenshots).
  • Latest price list and schedule, plus the 3 birthday party packages.
  • Access to the DomaiNesia account (for the domain) and, if available, Google Search Console and the old WordPress admin.
  • One contact person who collects feedback and gives approvals on behalf of The Dog Club.

How design approval works

  • The design is reviewed on a link, on both desktop and mobile.
  • Please send feedback as one combined list per round, within 3 working days.
  • The price includes 2 rounds of major revisions (layout, colours, fonts, imagery) and small text fixes at any time before launch.
  • Approval is given in writing, for example a WhatsApp message saying "Design approved".
  • Once approved, the design is locked so the build can start. Later changes to the approved design are quoted separately.
  • Delays in content or feedback shift the timeline by the same amount.
