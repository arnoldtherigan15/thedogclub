A day at the Club

The daily rhythm

Boarding dogs follow this routine. Daycare dogs join for the 9am – 5pm playtime.

  1. 7am

    Wake up at the Dog Villa, relaxed and ready for the day.

  2. 8am

    Morning beach walk with the gang along Batu Belig beach (Prestige boarding).

  3. 9am

    Boarding doggies are brought from the Villa to the Park. Let the fun begin!

  4. 9am – 5pm

    Playtime at the Club, all dogs supervised by our trained staff.

  5. 5pm

    Well-deserved feeding time at the Park.

  6. 6pm

    Quick inspection, then back to the Dog Villa for a good night's rest.

Weekends get busy.
