Issues with the current website

Audit of thedogclub.id, October 2026 (based on the latest accessible version).

Business & bookings

  • No links to Pawsi at all. The "Sign up" buttons on 6 pages still point to the old booking system (Gingr).
  • The server was not responding during the audit, so potential customers could not open the website.
  • Prices and schedules are images: Google can't read them, and they are hard to read and zoom on a phone.

SEO & performance

  • No meta description on 25 of 25 pages, so the snippets shown on Google can't be controlled.
  • The homepage title is just "The Dog Club", with no keywords such as dog hotel or dog daycare Bali.
  • Every page has 2–4 main headings (H1), which confuses Google about what the page is about.
  • Almost no images have alt text (0 of 36 on the homepage).
  • The homepage loads 25 JavaScript and 32 CSS files from 9 plugins, including an unused online shop and social media feeds. This makes the site slow.
  • The homepage has almost no text, which makes it weak in Google search.

Content & trust

  • The author's personal email is shown on every blog post and in the author page URL.
  • The Terms of Service are still a Shopify template with the wrong contact email.
  • WordPress's sample "Hello world!" post is still online.
  • Leftovers from the old online shop, typos, and different FAQ content on two pages.
