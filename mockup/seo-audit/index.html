Findings

For each issue: what we found, what we'll fix, and the impact you'll feel. Sorted by importance.

High

1. Sign-up buttons don't lead to Pawsi

What we found: the "Sign up" button on the homepage and the "Join the Club" buttons on the Pricing and Schedule pages link to the Gingr portal, and there is no link to Pawsi anywhere on the site, even though bookings are made on Pawsi. Visitors ready to book have to find their own way there.

What we'll fix: every booking button goes to Pawsi; service requests go to WhatsApp with a pre-filled message.

Impact you'll feel: More of the people who decide to book actually complete a booking, without having to search for where to book.

High

2. Locations can't be opened

What we found: the four location tiles on the homepage (Umalas, Ungasan, The Dog Hotel Berawa, Seseh) are screenshots, not buttons. Ungasan and Seseh have no link at all, and Umalas can only be reached through the logo. The menu's "Home" goes to the Umalas page while the logo goes to the homepage, so visitors land on different pages for the same click.

What we'll fix: one clear "Our locations" section with a card per location, each with its address, map and booking link, and a consistent menu.

Impact you'll feel: Customers find the location nearest to them in one tap, and Google can show each location for searches in its own area.

High

3. Prices and schedules are images

What we found: Google can't read text inside images, so searches like "dog daycare price Bali" can't match your pages. Images are also hard to read and zoom on a phone.

What we'll fix: all prices and hours are real text, on each service page and one combined pricing page.

Impact you'll feel: Your prices can show up in Google for price searches, and customers can check them quickly on their phone without asking on WhatsApp.

High

4. The homepage says almost nothing

What we found: the homepage is mostly images with a heading "Welcome to THE DOG CLUB". Google has very little to understand what you offer or where you are.

What we'll fix: clear copy about your services, Umalas location, prices, FAQs and a map.

Impact you'll feel: Google understands that you're a dog park, hotel and spa in Umalas, so you can start showing up for those searches.

Medium

5. No meta descriptions (0 of 25 pages)

What we found: this is the short text under your link on Google. Without it, Google picks random text, which usually gets fewer clicks.

What we'll fix: a written description for every page.

Impact you'll feel: Your Google listing shows a clear, inviting summary under each link, which earns more clicks for the same position.

Medium

6. Weak page titles

What we found: the homepage title is only "The Dog Club". Titles are one of the strongest ranking signals, and this one has no keywords such as dog hotel, daycare or Bali.

What we'll fix: e.g. "The Dog Club Bali — Dog Park, Hotel & Spa in Umalas".

Impact you'll feel: Each page can show up for the right search: "dog hotel Bali", "dog grooming Umalas", "dog daycare Canggu area".

Medium

7. 2–4 main headings (H1) per page

What we found: every page has a hidden "Welcome to THE DOG CLUB" main heading from the menu, and the Umalas page has four. Each page should have one main heading that says what it's about; several competing headings blur the topic.

What we'll fix: exactly one descriptive H1 per page.

Impact you'll feel: Every page has one clear topic, so Google knows which page to show for which search.

Medium

8. No structured data

What we found: structured data tells Google in a machine-readable way that you're a local business, with your address, hours and phone. None of the 25 pages has it.

What we'll fix: LocalBusiness data (address, hours, contact) and FAQ data.

Impact you'll feel: Your address, opening hours and phone number can appear directly in search results, and Google can link your website to your Maps listing.

Medium

9. Heavy, slow pages

What we found: the homepage loads about 15 JavaScript and 19 CSS files, four font families in 18 weights each, and the same Instagram stylesheet twice. Google ranks slow mobile sites lower, and visitors leave.

What we'll fix: no plugins, compressed images, Lighthouse target of 90+ on mobile.

Impact you'll feel: Pages open quickly on mobile data, fewer visitors give up while waiting, and Google stops penalising the site for speed.

Medium

10. Images have no alt text or names

What we found: none of the 15 images on the homepage has alt text, and file names like "Screenshot-2026-02-23-at-14.32.59.png" say nothing. Your photos can't appear in Google Images, and screen readers can't describe them.

What we'll fix: descriptive alt text and file names for every photo.

Impact you'll feel: Your photos of the park, pools and happy dogs can bring visitors from Google Images, and the site works for people using screen readers.

Low

11. No preview when shared on WhatsApp or Facebook

What we found: there are no Open Graph tags, so shared links show no image or proper title. Most of your sharing likely happens on WhatsApp and Instagram.

What we'll fix: a title, description and image for every shared link.

Impact you'll feel: Links shared on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook show a photo and a proper title, so more people tap them.

Low

12. Outdated and duplicate content

What we found: the footer still says © 2024 and "Kapubaten" (typo), the Terms of Service are a Shopify template with the wrong email, WordPress's sample "Hello world!" post was still online, and the FAQ appears in two different versions. This looks neglected to both Google and visitors.

What we'll fix: one up-to-date version of every page; the sample post removed.

Impact you'll feel: The site looks cared for and trustworthy, and Google no longer sees duplicate or empty pages.

Low

13. Personal email exposed

What we found: the blog author's personal email is shown on every article and in a page address, which invites spam.

What we'll fix: articles credited to "The Dog Club team".

Impact you'll feel: No more spam to a personal inbox, and articles look professional.

Low

14. An empty slider on the homepage

What we found: the slider at the bottom of the homepage has no content, so it shows as an empty space with dots.

What we'll fix: every section on the page has real content, and nothing is left empty.

Impact you'll feel: The homepage looks finished and cared for, from the first visit.

What's already fine

Every page has a canonical tag and a language setting, and the site uses HTTPS. These are kept in the new site.

What a website can and can't guarantee

Guaranteed

Every technical issue in this report is fixed, and every target above is met and shown to you in a before/after report.

Not guaranteed

A specific Google position. Rankings also depend on competitors, reviews and links from other sites. Nobody can honestly promise "page 1". A technically sound site gives you the best possible chance.

Beyond the website: the biggest wins for a local business

  • Google Business Profile. For searches like "dog hotel near me", the Google Maps listing often matters more than the website. Keep hours, photos and the website link up to date.
  • Google reviews. Ask happy customers for a review after their stay. The number and recency of reviews strongly influence local rankings.
  • Consistent details everywhere. Use the same name, address and phone number on the website, Google Maps, Instagram and directories.
  • Fresh content. An occasional blog post or updated photos signal an active business.

How we'll report progress

  1. Before launch
    Baseline: re-check the live site, measure its PageSpeed and Lighthouse scores, and record Google Search Console data.
  2. Launch day
    PageSpeed & Lighthouse scores of the new site, sitemap submitted to Google, redirects tested.
  3. +30 days
    Short report: pages indexed by Google, any errors, first changes in impressions and clicks.
  4. +90 days
    Before/after comparison of Google impressions and clicks (Search Console data takes a few weeks to settle).

The +90-day report requires access to Google Search Console. It is included with any active maintenance plan.
